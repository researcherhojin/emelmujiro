- `dompurify` `3.4.12` → `3.4.13` (GHSA-55q2-fjhq-7xh7, CWE-79, vulnerable `<=3.4.12`): the `IN_PLACE` hook removal left a detached subtree executable, an XSS in the one dependency whose job is preventing XSS. Unlike every other open advisory in this repo, this one **ships** — `dompurify` is a direct dep with its own vendor chunk (`vite.config.ts:88`) and backs `sanitizeBlogHtml.ts`, which every blog `content_html` passes through before `dangerouslySetInnerHTML`. Found by auditing CLAUDE.md's quantitative claims against reality: Gotcha #6 asserted `6 vulnerabilities (3 low, 2 moderate, 1 high)` and that "every one is a dev/CI tool that never ships", while `npm audit` reported 10 and one of them was shipping.
